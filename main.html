THEY HIDE BEHIND THE VEIL.

Bursting onto the scene with an immense following, NOVA//VOID have defied all expectations, and even written new ones through their eccentricity. Since 2028, they have thrown and hurled themselves to the forefront of the metal scene with a 'who dares wins' attitude to survive. Whether it's the ballad of 'NOTHING AFTER US' to make you weep, or the moshpit-maker of 'DEAD SIGNAL' raising your adrenaline, one thing remains unmistakably clear: they are here to stay.

NOT EVERYTHING UNDER THE LIGHT IS REAL.

The bigger question still remains, who exactly are these guys? And how can we be like them? So far, little result other than a fearful gaze has answered questions put to the members, leaving all we have to believe is their numbered jumpsuits they wear as clues of their origin. Some claim they are brothers, many claim they are fictitious in appearance, most agree that so long as the crucifying riffs and guttural cries for help continue, do we have to ask any questions?
